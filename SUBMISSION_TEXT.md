# Space Apps 2026 submission text (Team CodeHub)

## Project title
The Earth Information Jukebox: Hear NASA's Pictures of Earth

## Project summary
NASA explains our planet with colourful maps, but a colour bar means nothing to someone who cannot see it. The Earth Information Jukebox turns real NASA images into music that describes them, so blind and low-vision people, and anyone who learns by listening, can explore Earth science by ear. Our engine first reads what each colour means using NASA's own colour bar or legend. A line then sweeps the picture from west to east in 8 seconds, and the sound moves from your left ear to your right. Each region or kind of particle gets its own instrument, all notes stay on one pentatonic scale so it always sounds musical, and where there is nothing to report there is silence, so on the ocean map you can hear the shape of the continents. We built three tracks from NASA's Scientific Visualization Studio: ocean surface temperature, aerosols on 19 August 2025, and lightning seen from the International Space Station. Click any spot, or use the arrow keys, to hear its note and have the place name and value read aloud, for example "Arabian Sea, 29.4 °C". Accessible mode gives full keyboard control, speech and high contrast, and users can upload their own colour maps too. The temperatures our engine reads from the ocean image (28.4 °C at the equator, 2.5 °C near 60° south) follow the real ocean pattern, and all 28 automated checks pass.
