The Earth Information Jukebox

NASA pictures of Earth, played as music. Hear where it is warm, dusty or stormy.

Mode

Accessible mode is on. Everything works from the keyboard and is spoken aloud.

  • Space Play or pause the sound
  • Arrow keys Move around the picture and hear the place name and what is there. Hold Shift for bigger steps
  • W Where am I? Repeats the place, the value and the coordinates
  • 1 2 3 Choose a track
  • I Describe the image
  • S Describe the sound
  • H Hear this list of keys
  • V Turn the built-in voice off or on (turn it off if you use your own screen reader)
  • Esc Stop talking
  • A Go back to Standard mode

      How the image becomes music

      The engine first works out what each colour means, using NASA's own colour bar or legend for the picture (or, for an uploaded picture, a colour scale it learns from the picture itself). Then a line sweeps the picture from left to right. Time goes left to right, and the sound moves from your left ear to your right ear with it. The picture is split into bands from top to bottom, each with its own instrument. Higher values play higher notes, all on one pentatonic scale so they sound good together. Places with no data are silent, so the silences draw the shapes of the land or the empty sky. A soft click marks the start and end of the sweep.

      Images: NASA Scientific Visualization Studio. Place names: Natural Earth. Team CodeHub, NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026, "The Earth Information Jukebox".
